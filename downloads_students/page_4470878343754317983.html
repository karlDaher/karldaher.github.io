Behavior Analysis based on micro expressions

A micro-expression is a brief spontaneous facial movement that reveals a genuine emotion a person tries to hide. It is uncontrollable and therefore might provide consistent evidence for lie detection. In addition, the upper limit duration of a conventional micro-expression is 500 ms. Behavior Analysis Recognition based on micro-expressions would have several applications in the national security sector by providing an automatic micro-expression recognition. Since terrorism poses a threat to peace it has become imperative for nations and citizens to become more involved in recognizing and maybe preventing terrorist attacks.

Automatic facial expression recognition has been well developed using several different algorithms and the accuracy has reached over 90%. Antagonistically, automatic micro-expression detection has just recently started and small pieces of work have been available. Pfister et al. proposed a temporal interpolation method to recognize spontaneous facial expressions whereas Wu et al. employed Gabor features for feature extraction and GentleSVM as the classifier.
