## From research to practical applications

I’m Karl Daher. My work spans artificial intelligence, product development, and applied research, with a particular interest in empathy, human–computer interaction, and technology that supports people in everyday life.

My projects bring together academic research, industry collaboration, and independent work: from empathic conversational agents and digital mental health tools to real estate platforms, knowledge-sharing systems, and workflow automation.

[Explore my projects](/projects/){: .btn} [Get in touch](/contact/){: .btn}

## Industry & product development

At IMMOMIG SA, my work included the AI feature track for **DigiRENT-AI** and contributions to **dreamo.ch**, connecting applied AI with real estate workflows. Other projects include **Colearnis**, a platform for capturing and sharing employee know-how, and **My CSIR**, a secure messaging and administrative portal.

[Browse these projects and their outcomes →](/projects/)

## Research & development

My research focuses on affective computing, empathic systems, and conversational AI. My publications cover empathic chatbot responses, emotion recognition, stress support, and human–computer interaction. My research work also includes the **TIMED Europe** collaboration on time experience in the digital age, alongside digital health collaborations with the University of Fribourg.

[Read my publications →](/publications/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QX-OMOoAAAAJ&hl=en) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Karl_Daher3)
