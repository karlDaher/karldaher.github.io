This seminar explores emotions and empathy in interactive technologies, with a focus on recognizing human emotions, especially stress, through facial expressions and physiological signals. Students examine advances in emotion recognition and empathic behaviour change, evaluate methods, and develop empathic companions and practical applications for stress reduction and well-being.
