This course integrates user experience into the design of innovative interactive systems. It covers interaction design, usability metrics, design rules, and methods for measuring user experience. Practical sessions explore user-friendly interactions and emerging technologies such as voice and gestures, preparing students for the conception phase of interactive systems.
