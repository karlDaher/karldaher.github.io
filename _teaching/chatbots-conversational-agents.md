This seminar explores the evolution of chatbots from transactional exchanges to conversations incorporating emotions and empathy. It covers design approaches, technologies, psychological theories, natural language understanding, and language generation. Practical tasks include examining existing chatbots and developing concepts involving mood, personality, and language generation.
